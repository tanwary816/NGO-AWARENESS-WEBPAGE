About Our NGO


InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded in 2020. The NGO works towards education, women empowerment, food distribution, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and skill development across India.


Our mission is to empower communities and create a better future through compassion, awareness, and social action.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Bachpanshala

Providing quality education, digital literacy, and learning support for underprivileged children.

Project Seva

Distributing food, clothes, and essential supplies to needy families and homeless people.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development, education, and financial independence programs.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental awareness through tree plantation drives and sustainability initiatives.
